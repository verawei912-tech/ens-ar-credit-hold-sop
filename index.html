AR Aging Escalation & Credit Insurance Reporting Timeline Strict Compliance Window
All aging milestones are measured strictly from the invoice Due Date (NEVER the Invoice Date). Monitor Acumatica AR Aging reports routinely and whenever customers place new orders.
Day 0 • Due Date

Aging Clock Starts

  • Calculated from Due Date, never Invoice Date.
  • Track Balance and First Due Date in Acumatica AR Aging report.
Day 30+ • Calendar Days

Credit Hold Release Gate

  • Not on Extension List → Ineligible / Auto Reject.
  • On Extension List → Eligible for submission to Steve / Rock / Rui (not auto-approved).
Day 60+ • Internal Alert

Sales & Management Alert

  • Immediately notify Sales, Sales Manager, and Dylan.
  • Push aggressive collection before Day 90.
  • For vague payment plans (e.g., Eagle I), draft a formal deadline warning email and get Yoga's review before sending.
Day 90+ & > $10,000

Insurance Reporting

  • Two mandatory conditions (BOTH required): Past Due > 90 days AND Overdue Amount > $10,000.
  • Filing Deadline: Report to the credit insurer within 30 days after Day 90 (Day 90–120 window).
  • Bankruptcy Exception: Report immediately regardless of days or amount.
Key Distinction: If a customer is over 90 days past due but their overdue balance is $10,000 or less (and there is no bankruptcy), credit insurance reporting is not required — continue standard internal collection procedures.
